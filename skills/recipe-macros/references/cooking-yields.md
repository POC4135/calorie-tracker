# Cooking yields

Typical ratios of cooked weight to raw weight. Use them to sanity-check a user's serving count, or to estimate a cooked weight when nobody weighed the pot. Real yields vary with water, pot, and cooking time, so a weighed cooked weight always wins.

| Raw ingredient | Cooked to | Cooked weight vs raw | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| White rice (basmati, sona masoori) | steamed | 2.8-3.2x | 1 cup raw (185 g) makes about 3 cups cooked |
| Brown rice | steamed | 2.5-2.8x | |
| Toor, moong or masoor dal | thick dal | 4-5x | 1 cup raw (about 200 g) makes about 4 cups |
| Toor, moong or masoor dal | thin everyday dal | 5.5-7x | about 5-6 cups per cup raw |
| Whole urad and rajma (dal makhani) | simmered | 4-5x | |
| Chickpeas or rajma (dry) | boiled and drained | 2.2-2.5x | 1 cup dry makes about 2.5 cups cooked beans |
| Chana dal | boiled | 2.2-2.5x | |
| Poha (thick) | soaked and cooked | 2-2.3x | |
| Sooji (upma) | cooked | 3.5-4.5x | |
| Oats | cooked in water | 5-6x | |
| Soya chunks | soaked and squeezed | 2.5-3x | |
| Chicken, boneless | curry or grilled | 0.70-0.80x | loses 20-30% water |
| Chicken, curry cut (bone in) | curry | about 0.75x | weigh meat raw without bones |
| Goat or lamb | slow-cooked curry | 0.60-0.70x | |
| Onions | browned for masala | 0.40-0.60x | longer browning, more loss |
| Spinach | wilted | 0.10-0.15x | a 340 g bunch wilts to about 40-50 g |
| Okra, eggplant, cauliflower | sauteed | 0.65-0.85x | |
| Potatoes | boiled | about 1x | |

## Household yields

- One 6-7 in roti uses about 30 g atta; a pressure cooker of dal for a family of four usually starts with 3/4 to 1 cup raw dal.
- A katori of dal or curry is about 150 ml; a cup is 240 ml. Cooked dal and curry weigh close to 1 g per ml.
- A 3-litre pressure cooker of thin dal from 1 cup raw yields about 1.3-1.5 kg.
