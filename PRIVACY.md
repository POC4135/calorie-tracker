# Privacy

Katori is a set of instructions, a food database, and a small Python calculator. It has no server, no account, no analytics, and no database.

## What Katori does with your data

- **Sends nothing anywhere.** The calculator makes no network calls. It reads only the CSV files that ship with the skills and prints results back to Claude.
- **Stores nothing.** There is no log file or history. Running totals exist only as text in your conversation.
- **Uses the web only when you ask for something that needs it.** For a chain restaurant's official nutrition, Claude may run a web search with its own tools, under your account's settings. Katori never sends your meals, weight, or targets to a website.

## What Claude does with your data

Your messages, photos, and Claude's replies are handled by Anthropic like any other Claude conversation, under your account's privacy settings and Anthropic's privacy policy. If Claude's memory is turned on for your account, Claude may remember details such as your targets between chats; you control that in Claude's settings.

## Health information

Weight, body measurements, and diet are personal. Share only what you are comfortable sharing in a Claude conversation. Katori works without a BMR or exact measurements; the targets will just be less tailored.
