# Indian Food Nutritional Database

Comprehensive database of Indian dishes with portion sizes and macro breakdowns. Organized by category and region.

## Usage Notes

- **Portions**: Standard home-cooked or restaurant serving sizes
- **Variations**: Adjust for cooking method, ingredients, regional differences
- **Accuracy**: ±15% margin, influenced by preparation specifics
- **Restaurant multiplier**: Add 25-50% calories for restaurant versions (extra oil/ghee/cream). See US Restaurant and Takeout Adjustments below.

## Cooking Method Adjustments

### Fried vs Steamed
- **Fried**: +40-60% calories due to oil absorption
- **Example**: Steamed idli (39 kcal) vs fried vada (145 kcal)

### Restaurant vs Home
- **Restaurant**: +20-30% calories (extra oil, ghee, cream, butter)
- **Example**: Home dal (115 kcal) vs restaurant dal tadka (180 kcal)

### Ghee vs Oil
- **Ghee**: 112 kcal per tbsp
- **Oil (any)**: 120 kcal per tbsp
- **Usage**: Typical tadka uses 1-2 tbsp, rotis use 0.5 tbsp each

### Portion Size Variations
- **Small**: 80% of listed portion
- **Large**: 130% of listed portion
- **Restaurant**: Usually 1.2-1.5x home portions

## Regional Variations

### North Indian
- Higher fat content (ghee, cream, butter)
- Richer gravies (butter chicken, paneer makhani)
- Bread-heavy (rotis, parathas, naans)

### South Indian
- Lower fat (steaming common, coconut oil)
- Rice-based (dosa, idli, rice dishes)
- Fermented foods (dosa, idli batter)

### Bengali
- Mustard oil usage (different flavor, same calories as other oils)
- Fish-heavy (lower fat than meat)
- Sweets (rasgulla, sandesh)

### Gujarati
- Slight sweetness in savory dishes (+2-3g sugar)
- Dhokla, thepla (steamed, lower fat)
- Ghee usage similar to North Indian

## Estimation Guidelines

When user provides:

1. **Dish name only**: Use standard portions from this database
2. **Portion in grams**: Calculate proportionally from database values
3. **"Small/medium/large"**: Apply 80% / 100% / 130% multipliers
4. **"Restaurant"**: Apply 1.25x to home-cooked values (1.5x for makhani, korma, malai, shahi, butter dishes), plus US portion sizes from US Restaurant and Takeout Adjustments below
5. **"Fried" modifier**: Add 50% to base calories
6. **Multiple items**: Sum individual items, don't average

## Confidence Scoring

- **High (>70%)**: Exact database match, portion specified in grams
- **Medium (40-70%)**: Database match, portion estimated (small/medium/large)
- **Low (<40%)**: No database match, visual estimate only, unusual preparation

## US Restaurant and Takeout Adjustments

US Indian restaurant portions are larger than home portions in this file.

- **Takeout curry container**: typically about 2 cups. Default to 2 cups per entree unless the user says it was shared, then apply the restaurant multiplier below on top of the home value per cup.
- **Restaurant multiplier**: 1.25-1.5x home calories for curries (extra oil, butter, cream). Use 1.5x for makhani, korma, malai, shahi and butter dishes.
- **Naan**: US restaurant naan is often 100-140g. Plain ~300-380 kcal, butter/garlic ~350-450 kcal.
- **Rice with a takeout order**: usually 1.5-2 cups cooked (300-420 kcal).
- **Masala dosa (US restaurant)**: often 1.5-2x the listed size; ~400-550 kcal.
- **Samosa (US restaurant/grocery)**: often 100-130g; ~320-420 kcal each.
- **Buffet plate**: estimate each item separately; assume restaurant multiplier on all gravies.
- **Packaged frozen Indian meals**: always prefer the label.
