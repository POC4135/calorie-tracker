# Disclaimer

Katori provides general nutrition estimates for informational purposes. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it is not a substitute for a doctor, registered dietitian, or other qualified professional.

- Calorie and macro values are estimates. Real foods vary, and portion estimates from descriptions or photos can be off by 20-30% or more.
- Targets come from population equations and published guidelines. Individual needs differ, and medical conditions and medications change them.
- Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before changing your diet if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, are under 18, have diabetes, kidney disease, heart disease, or a history of disordered eating, or take medication that affects appetite or blood sugar.
- If you are struggling with food, eating, or body image, you deserve support. In the US, the National Alliance for Eating Disorders helpline is 1-866-662-1235 (weekdays 9 am to 7 pm Eastern). Outside the US, contact a doctor or a local helpline. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
